Privacy Policy

Voyage Runner · SmartInc Tanzania · Effective 12 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Voyage Runner game ("the game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. "We", "us" and "our" mean SmartInc Tanzania, the publisher of the game.

2. Services that process your information

The game is built with services from these companies. They process information on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • Unity Technologies — Unity Authentication (player accounts and sign-in), Cloud Save (game progress), Leaderboards (names and scores) and Unity Ads (rewarded video ads). Unity Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase — Crashlytics (crash reports: device model, operating system, app version and what the game was doing when it crashed) and Cloud Messaging (a notification token, so the game can send notifications you have allowed). Firebase Analytics is switched off, and Firebase does not collect the advertising ID. Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google or Apple — only if you choose to sign in with them, through Unity Player Accounts. Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy
  • Meta (Facebook) — for Facebook sign-in if you choose it (players 13 and over only). For players 13 and over, the Facebook SDK also records app events (such as app launches) and may collect the device advertising ID, which Meta uses to measure and improve advertising. None of this happens for players under 13, or before the age question is answered. Meta Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Ads

The game offers optional rewarded videos (for example "Watch ad · Claim ×2"). Ads are provided by Unity Ads. For players 13 and over, Unity Ads may use the advertising ID to show and measure ads. For players under 13, ads are child-directed and not personalised, and are not based on the advertising ID or on what the player does in other apps. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

4. What other players can see

Your chosen player name and your scores appear on the public leaderboards. Choose a name that doesn't identify you if you prefer to stay anonymous. Player names that contain offensive words are blocked.

5. How long we keep information

We keep your account, progress and scores while your account exists. Weekly leaderboards are archived after each week so weekly rewards can be paid. When you delete your account, your account and saved progress are deleted from our services (see below); backups and records kept by the service providers are removed on their normal schedules.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the game: Home → Account (person icon) → Delete account, then tap again to confirm. This deletes your account and cloud-saved progress straight away.
  • Without the game: follow the steps on our account deletion page.

7. Children

Voyage Runner is made for players aged 9 and over, including children, and we follow the Google Play Families policy.

  • Age question: the first time the game opens, it asks for the player's year of birth. The answer stays on the phone and is not sent to us. Until it is answered, every player is treated as a child.
  • Players under 13: get only child-directed, non-personalised ads; the advertising ID is not used for them; Facebook sign-in and Facebook app events are turned off. They can still play as a guest or sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Player names: names shown on the leaderboards are checked against a list of blocked words. We encourage players not to use their real name.

We do not knowingly collect more information from children than the game needs to work. If you are a parent and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and stored by the service providers listed above, who use industry-standard protections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your information, or to object to some uses of it. To make a request, email us at the address below. You can also delete your account yourself as described above.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be announced in the game.

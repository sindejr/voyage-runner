Delete your Voyage Runner account

Voyage Runner · SmartInc Tanzania

Option 1 — in the game (instant)

  1. Open Voyage Runner.
  2. On the home screen, tap the Account button (person icon, top right).
  3. Tap Delete account, then tap again to confirm.

Your account and cloud-saved progress are deleted straight away, and the game starts fresh as a new guest.

Option 2 — by email (no game needed)

Email CONTACT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my Voyage Runner account" and include:

  • Your player name as shown on the leaderboard, and
  • How you signed in (Google, Apple, Facebook or guest) and the email of that account, if any.

We'll confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account and the link to any Google, Apple or Facebook sign-in
  • Cloud-saved progress: coins, gems, keys, runners, upgrades, missions and season progress
  • Your player name and scores on the leaderboards

What may be kept

  • Archived weekly leaderboards, which are removed on the service provider's schedule (normally within 90 days)
  • Records that service providers (Unity, Meta, Google, Apple) must keep for security, fraud prevention or legal reasons, under their own policies

Progress stored only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the game.
